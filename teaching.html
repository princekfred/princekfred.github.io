Teaching & Service

I support students through teaching and research mentorship, and volunteer in STEM outreach and community programs.

01

Laboratory instruction

Introductory Chemistry Laboratory

As a graduate teaching assistant for CHEM 115L at the University of North Dakota, I support undergraduate students as they practice experimental techniques, laboratory safety, and chemical reasoning.

02

Research mentorship

Undergraduate researchers

I mentor undergraduate researchers, including Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) students in our group, supporting their development in quantum algorithms, computational tools, and scientific problem-solving.

03

Scientific outreach

Physics and quantum communities

I serve as an APS Student Ambassador and a Qiskit Advocate, sharing physics and quantum computing with students and wider audiences.
